🚀 Getting Started

X2Streaming-ASR is built for people, not for programmers. There are no commands to type and no configuration files to edit. The application listens to audio from your microphone, processes it as a continuous stream, and turns it into readable text while you are still speaking.

The first time you open the application, it will ask for permission to use your microphone. Approve the request, pick the microphone you want to use from the list, and start talking. Text appears in the main window within a fraction of a second, which is what makes the streaming approach so comfortable for live note-taking, captioning, and dictation.

Because X2Streaming-ASR works continuously rather than in short recorded chunks, you never have to pause between sentences. Speak naturally, and the transcript keeps up with you.

🛠️ Troubleshooting

Most first-time questions have a quick answer. Work through these points if something does not behave as expected.

  • No text appears: check that the correct microphone is selected and that the small input meter moves when you speak.
  • The transcript is patchy: move closer to the microphone and reduce background noise such as fans or open windows.
  • Windows blocks microphone access: open Settings, go to Privacy, then Microphone, and make sure app access is switched on.
  • The download does not start: refresh the release page in your browser and try again, or use a different browser.
